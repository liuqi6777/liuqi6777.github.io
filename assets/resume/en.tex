\begin{rSection}{Research Directions}
\textbf{I. LLM for Search}

\textbf{Multi-vector retrieval.} My early \workref{p08}{model analysis} and \workref{p07}{matching and pruning study} explored how multi-vector retrieval balances effectiveness and efficiency. I also led development of the open-source long-context late-interaction retriever \href{https://huggingface.co/jinaai/jina-colbert-v1-en}{jina-colbert}.

\textbf{Dense retrieval models.} From the bilingual \href{https://huggingface.co/jinaai/jina-embeddings-v2-base-zh}{jina-embeddings-v2} model, I moved toward broader uses of embeddings: \workref{p03}{E2Rank} extends them to listwise reranking, and \workref{p01}{Learning to Retrieve} explores reinforcement learning in embedding space.

\textbf{LLM for reranking.} This line spans efficient listwise ranking \workref{p06}{with passage embeddings}, reusable methods in \workref{p05}{LLM4Ranking}, \workref{p04}{relevance interpretation}, and the diffusion-based approach \workref{p02}{DiffuRank}.

\medskip
\textbf{II. Search Agents}

\textbf{Behavior and information acquisition.} I analyze how long-horizon agents search and where they fail in \workref{p11}{Diagnosing Search Behavior}; \workref{p10}{Fetch-then-Explore} then separates source selection from extraction over a persistent workspace.

\textbf{Search as Code (ongoing).} I am developing \workref{p09}{OpenSaC}, a Python interface that lets deep-research agents organize and execute search through code.
\end{rSection}
